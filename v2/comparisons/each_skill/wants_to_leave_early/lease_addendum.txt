APARTMENT LEASE

Lakeview Terrace Apartments, 1840 SW 13th Street, Gainesville, Florida 32608

1. PARTIES. This lease is between Marsh Creek Property Management, LLC, as agent for the owner of Lakeview Terrace Apartments ("Landlord"), and Jordan Reyes ("Tenant").

2. UNIT. Landlord rents to Tenant Apartment 12 at Lakeview Terrace Apartments, 1840 SW 13th Street, Gainesville, Florida 32608.

3. TERM. This lease begins on August 1, 2025 and ends on July 31, 2026.

4. RENT. Rent is $1,050.00 per month, due on the first day of each month, paid through the resident portal or by check delivered to the office.

5. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant has paid a security deposit of $1,050.00. Landlord holds it in a separate non-interest-bearing account at First Gainesville Savings Bank, 2200 NW 13th Street, Gainesville, Florida 32609. Tenant is not entitled to interest on the deposit. The deposit may not be used for the last month's rent.

6. MOVE-OUT. At move-out Tenant shall leave the apartment clean and empty, return all keys to the office, and give Landlord a forwarding address in writing.

7. NOTICES TO LANDLORD. Written notices to Landlord shall be delivered by hand to the office at 1840 SW 13th Street, Suite A, Gainesville, Florida 32608, or sent there by certified mail.

8. NOTICES TO TENANT. Notices to Tenant shall be delivered to the apartment while Tenant lives there, and after move-out shall be mailed to the forwarding address Tenant gives in writing. Landlord and Tenant have not agreed to deliver notices by e-mail.

9. HOLDING OVER. If Tenant stays after July 31, 2026 with Landlord's written consent, the tenancy becomes month to month on the same terms.

10. ENTIRE AGREEMENT. This lease is the whole agreement. It may be changed only in writing signed by Landlord and Tenant.

11. EARLY TERMINATION. Tenant may end this lease before July 31, 2026 by giving Landlord 30 days' written notice, delivered as clause 7 says, and paying the amount Tenant agreed to in Addendum A.
