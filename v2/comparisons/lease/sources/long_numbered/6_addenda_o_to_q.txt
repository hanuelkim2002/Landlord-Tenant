# ADDENDUM O
# HURRICANE AND SEVERE WEATHER ADDENDUM

This addendum is part of the Individual Lease Agreement between Archer Commons Owner, LLC and Riley M. Torres for Bedroom C, Unit 3108.

## O-1. Why This Addendum Matters

Gainesville is inland, but hurricanes and tropical storms can still bring high winds, heavy rain, flooding, falling trees, and long power outages to the Community. This addendum explains what we will do and what you must do before, during, and after a storm.

## O-2. Before a Storm

O-2.1. Announcements. When the National Weather Service issues a tropical storm or hurricane watch for Alachua County, we will send residents an announcement by email and text message with the Community's storm plan, the hours of the leasing office, and the emergency maintenance number.

O-2.2. Balconies and Patios. Within twelve (12) hours after a watch is issued, you must bring all furniture, plants, bicycles, and other items inside from your balcony. Items left outside may be removed by our staff and stored until after the storm, and we are not responsible for loss or damage to them.

O-2.3. Vehicles. If the Community's storm plan says so, you must move your vehicle from the surface lots to the parking garage, or away from trees, as directed by staff.

O-2.4. Supplies. We recommend that you keep at least three days of drinking water, food that does not need cooking, flashlights, batteries, a first aid kit, and any medicine you need. Candles may not be used for light (Addendum A, Section A-5.1).

O-2.5. Your Plans. If you plan to leave Gainesville before a storm, tell your Roommates and update your emergency contact in the resident portal. Turn off the breakers for the water heater and the range before you leave, and leave the air conditioning running at 80 degrees.

## O-3. Evacuation

O-3.1. Orders. If public officials order an evacuation that covers the Community, you must leave as ordered. We may close the Community during the evacuation, and staff will not be on site.

O-3.2. Rent During Evacuation. An evacuation order does not by itself end the Lease or reduce your rent. If the Unit is damaged so that it cannot be lived in, Section 31.2 of the Lease applies.

O-3.3. Students. Your school may close its campus or move classes online during a storm. A school closure does not change your duties under the Lease.

## O-4. During a Storm

Stay inside the building and away from windows and glass doors. Do not go onto balconies, into the courtyard, or into the parking garage during high winds. Do not use elevators if the power is flickering. Do not run a portable generator anywhere in the Community.

## O-5. After a Storm

O-5.1. Reporting Damage. Report any damage to the Unit, including water coming in through windows, doors, or ceilings, through the resident portal as soon as it is safe to do so. Take photographs of any damage to your belongings for your insurance claim.

O-5.2. Repairs. After a storm, repairs will be made in order of urgency: first conditions that threaten life or safety, then conditions that let water in, then everything else. Response times in Section 13.3 of the Lease may be longer.

O-5.3. Power Outages. We are not responsible for food spoiled during a power outage caused by the utility or by a storm. Power outages caused by the utility are not an interruption of service by us.

O-5.4. Common Areas. Amenities may stay closed after a storm until they are inspected and repaired.

## O-6. Mold After Water Damage

If water enters the Unit during a storm, remove wet items from the floor, run the air conditioning and fans, and report the water at once so that we can dry the Unit and prevent mold (Addendum E).

Resident's Initials: R.M.T.

# ADDENDUM P
# AMENITY USE AND RELEASE

This addendum is part of the Individual Lease Agreement between Archer Commons Owner, LLC and Riley M. Torres for Bedroom C, Unit 3108.

## P-1. Use at Your Own Risk

P-1.1. Voluntary Use. Your use of the pool, fitness center, courtyard, grills, game room, study lounges, and other amenities is voluntary. You agree to follow the posted rules and the Community Policies, and to use the amenities only for their intended purposes.

P-1.2. Health. You should talk to a doctor before beginning an exercise program. Stop exercising and get help at once if you feel faint, dizzy, short of breath, or have chest pain.

P-1.3. Release. To the extent Florida law allows, you release Owner and Manager from claims for injury to you or your guests arising from the ordinary use of the amenities, except claims caused by our negligence or intentional misconduct. This release does not apply to any duty we have under Section 83.51, Florida Statutes.

## P-2. Pool Rules

P-2.1. No Lifeguard. No lifeguard is on duty. Children under 14 must be with an adult at all times.

P-2.2. Swimming Attire. Proper swimwear is required in the pool. Cut-off jeans and street clothes are not allowed in the water.

P-2.3. Safety. No diving, running, pushing, or rough play. No glass, food, or alcohol in the pool area. Music must be played through headphones.

P-2.4. Weather. The pool closes when thunder is heard or lightning is seen, and stays closed until thirty (30) minutes after the last thunder or lightning.

P-2.5. Capacity. The pool area may hold no more than the number of people posted at the gate.

## P-3. Fitness Center Rules

P-3.1. Residents Only. The fitness center is for residents only. You must use your own access card to enter, and you may not let anyone else in.

P-3.2. Equipment. Use equipment only as intended. Return weights to the racks. Report broken equipment to the leasing office or through the resident portal.

P-3.3. Time Limits. When others are waiting, limit cardio equipment use to thirty (30) minutes.

P-3.4. Personal Trainers. Outside personal trainers may not train residents in the fitness center.

## P-4. Courtyard Grills

P-4.1. Hours. The courtyard gas grills may be used from 10:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m.

P-4.2. Cleaning. Clean the grill after use and turn off the gas at the valve.

P-4.3. Reservations. Grills cannot be reserved, except for Community events.

## P-5. Clubhouse Reservations

P-5.1. Reservations. Residents may reserve the clubhouse event room for a private gathering of no more than twenty (20) people by request through the resident portal at least seven (7) days ahead.

P-5.2. Deposit. A refundable reservation deposit of $100.00 is required, and is returned within seven (7) days after the event if the room is left clean and undamaged.

P-5.3. Rules. No alcohol may be served at a reserved event unless every guest is at least 21 years old and a resident has signed the alcohol acknowledgment form. Events must end by 11:00 p.m.

## P-6. Suspension of Amenity Privileges

If you or your guests violate the amenity rules, we may suspend your access to the amenity for up to thirty (30) days by written notice. A suspension does not reduce your rent or the amenity fee.

Resident's Initials: R.M.T.

# ADDENDUM Q
# COMMUNITY TECHNOLOGY AND PRIVACY ADDENDUM

This addendum is part of the Individual Lease Agreement between Archer Commons Owner, LLC and Riley M. Torres for Bedroom C, Unit 3108.

## Q-1. Video Cameras in Common Areas

Q-1.1. Where Cameras Are. The Community uses video cameras in common areas, including building entrances, hallways, the parking garage, the mail room, the pool gate, and the clubhouse. There are no cameras inside any unit or bedroom.

Q-1.2. Use of Recordings. Recordings are used to investigate incidents, damage, and violations of the Lease and Community Policies, and may be given to law enforcement. Cameras are not monitored at all times, and their use does not mean the Community is secure (Section 36.1 of the Lease).

Q-1.3. Your Cameras. You may not install a camera that records any common area, a Roommate's bedroom, or a shared bathroom, or that records audio of other people without their consent.

## Q-2. Access Cards and Smart Locks

Q-2.1. Entry Records. Building and amenity doors use electronic access cards. The system records the date and time each card is used. We use these records to manage access, investigate incidents, and confirm the date you return your card at move-out.

Q-2.2. Unit Door Lock. The Unit's front door has an electronic lock that opens with your access card or the resident app. Staff entry into the Unit is also recorded by the lock.

## Q-3. Community Wi-Fi

Q-3.1. Network. The Community provides wireless internet in each unit and in the common areas. Each resident receives a private network login.

Q-3.2. Personal Routers. Personal wireless routers may interfere with the Community network and may not be used unless staff approve them.

Q-3.3. Gaming and Streaming Devices. Gaming consoles, smart televisions, and streaming devices may be registered on the network through the resident portal.

Q-3.4. Support. Internet problems should be reported to the internet provider's 24-hour support line posted in the resident portal, not to maintenance.

## Q-4. Resident App and Portal

Q-4.1. Account. Your resident portal account is personal. Do not share your password.

Q-4.2. Messages. We may send you announcements, payment reminders, package notices, and maintenance updates through the app, by text message, or by email. You may turn off marketing messages, but not messages about your Lease, payments, or safety.

## Q-5. Your Information

Q-5.1. What We Collect. We keep your application, your Lease, your payment history, your maintenance requests, your access records, and your contact information.

Q-5.2. Sharing. We share your information only with Owner, with service providers who help us manage the Community, with your guarantor about amounts you owe, with law enforcement when required, and as the law otherwise requires.

Q-5.3. Keeping Records. We keep your records for at least five (5) years after your Lease ends.

Resident's Initials: R.M.T.
