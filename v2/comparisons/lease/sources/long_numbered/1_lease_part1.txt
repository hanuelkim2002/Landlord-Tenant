In this Lease, "we," "us," and "our" mean Owner and Manager, and "you" and "your" mean Resident. Please read the whole Lease, including every addendum. The addenda listed in Section 38 are part of this Lease.

## 2. PREMISES AND BEDROOM ASSIGNMENT

2.1. The Community. Archer Commons Student Residences is an apartment community located at 3800 SW Archer Road, Gainesville, Alachua County, Florida 32608 (the "Community"). The Community includes the apartment buildings, the clubhouse, the parking garage, the surface parking lots, the pool and courtyard, the fitness center, the study lounges, and all other common areas.

2.2. Your Bedroom and Unit. You are leasing the individual bedroom identified as Bedroom C (the "Bedroom") in apartment Unit 3108 (the "Unit"), a four-bedroom, four-bathroom apartment. You have the exclusive right to use the Bedroom and its attached bathroom, and the right to use the Unit's living room, kitchen, and laundry closet in common with the other residents of the Unit (your "Roommates"), each of whom has a separate lease with us.

2.3. Furniture. The Unit is furnished. The Bedroom includes a full-size bed frame and mattress, a desk and desk chair, a dresser, and a nightstand. The common areas of the Unit include a sofa, a lounge chair, a coffee table, an entertainment stand, four bar stools, a refrigerator, a range, a microwave, a dishwasher, and a full-size washer and dryer. Furniture may not be removed from the Unit or moved from one room to another without our written consent.

2.4. Reassignment. We may reassign you to a different bedroom or unit of similar size and price in the Community if we must do so to make repairs, to resolve a documented conflict between Roommates, or to consolidate vacant bedrooms, after giving you at least seven (7) days' written notice. A reassignment does not change your rent or any other term of this Lease, and we will pay reasonable moving costs if we reassign you for our own convenience.

2.5. No Right to a Particular Roommate. Roommates are assigned by us. We do not promise that any particular person will be your Roommate, and you are not released from this Lease because of a change in Roommates or a vacancy in the Unit.

## 3. LEASE TERM

3.1. Term. The term of this Lease begins at 10:00 a.m. on August 15, 2026 (the "Move-In Date") and ends at 12:00 noon on July 31, 2027 (the "End Date"), unless ended earlier as this Lease provides.

3.2. Fixed Term. This Lease is for a fixed term. It does not renew automatically, and it does not continue month to month after the End Date. If you want to live in the Community for the next lease year, you must sign a new lease.

3.3. Delay of Occupancy. If we cannot give you possession of the Bedroom on the Move-In Date because of construction, repairs, or a prior resident's holdover, your rent will be reduced by one-thirtieth (1/30) of the monthly installment for each day of delay, and we will provide temporary housing at no cost to you. If the delay lasts more than fourteen (14) days, you may end this Lease by written notice to us before possession is offered, and we will refund all rent, fees, and deposits you have paid.

3.4. Early Move-In. If we allow you to move in before the Move-In Date, you will pay $30.00 per day for each day of early occupancy, and all terms of this Lease will apply during that time.

## 4. RENT AND INSTALLMENTS

4.1. Total Rent. The total rent for the Lease term is $10,788.00 ("Total Rent"). Total Rent is a single amount for the whole term. For your convenience, you may pay Total Rent in twelve (12) monthly installments of $899.00 each.

4.2. Due Date. The first installment is due on or before the Move-In Date and is not prorated. Each later installment is due on or before the first (1st) day of each month from September 2026 through July 2027. You must pay each installment on time and in full, without any deduction, and without demand by us.

4.3. Rent Is Per Bedroom. Your rent is for your Bedroom and your share of the common areas only. You are not responsible for the rent of any Roommate, and no Roommate is responsible for yours.

4.4. Other Charges Are Additional Rent. All charges you owe under this Lease other than installments of Total Rent, including late charges, utility overage charges, damage charges, and service charges, are "Additional Rent." Additional Rent is due on the first day of the month after we bill it, unless this Lease says otherwise.

4.5. How We Apply Payments. We will apply each payment first to unpaid Additional Rent, then to unpaid installments of Total Rent, in the order they became due, no matter what is written on the payment.

## 5. LATE CHARGES AND RETURNED PAYMENTS

5.1. Late Charges. If any installment is not received in full by 11:59 p.m. on the third (3rd) day of the month, you will owe an initial late charge of $50.00 on the fourth (4th) day of the month, plus a daily late charge of $10.00 for each additional day the installment remains unpaid. Daily late charges will not be charged for more than fifteen (15) days in any month. Late charges are Additional Rent. The parties agree that late charges are a reasonable estimate of our costs from late payment, which are hard to determine.

5.2. Returned Payments. You will pay $35.00 for each payment returned unpaid for any reason, in addition to any late charges. After two returned payments, we may require you to pay all future amounts by cashier's check or money order.

5.3. No Waiver. Accepting a late or partial payment does not waive our right to collect the rest, to charge late charges, or to give you any notice Florida law allows.

## 6. PAYMENT METHODS

6.1. Resident Portal. You may pay rent and other charges online through the Blue Heron resident portal or mobile app by electronic check (ACH) or by debit or credit card. Card payments are processed by a third-party processor, which may charge you its own processing fee.

6.2. Other Methods. You may also pay by personal check, cashier's check, or money order delivered to the leasing office during office hours or placed in the leasing office drop box. We do not accept cash.

6.3. When Payment Is Received. A payment made through the portal is received when the portal confirms it. A payment left in the drop box after 5:00 p.m. is received on the next business day.

## 7. SECURITY DEPOSIT

7.1. Amount. You have paid us a security deposit of $450.00 (the "Deposit"). The Deposit secures your performance of this Lease. It is not a limit on what you may owe us, and you may not use it to pay any installment of Total Rent.

7.2. How the Deposit Is Held. We hold the Deposit in a separate non-interest-bearing account at Ameris Bank, 3700 SW Archer Road, Gainesville, Florida 32608, for the benefit of residents. We do not commingle the Deposit with our other funds, and we do not pay interest on it.

7.3. Return of the Deposit. After you move out, we will return the Deposit, or give you written notice of our intent to impose a claim on it, within the time and in the manner required by Section 83.49, Florida Statutes. You must give us your forwarding address in writing so that we can send you notices about the Deposit.

7.4. Joint Charges. If damage to the common areas of the Unit is not caused by an identified resident, we may divide the cost of repair equally among the residents of the Unit and charge each resident's share against that resident's deposit.

## 8. FEES

8.1. Application Fee. You paid a non-refundable application fee of $50.00 when you applied.

8.2. Administrative Fee. You paid a non-refundable administrative fee of $200.00 when you signed this Lease. It pays for processing your application, preparing your Bedroom, and setting up your account. It is not a deposit and will not be returned.

8.3. Amenity Fee. You will pay a non-refundable amenity fee of $250.00 with your first installment. It pays for the operation of the pool, fitness center, study lounges, and resident events during the Lease term. The amenity fee is not reduced if any amenity is closed for repairs, weather, or a public health order.

8.4. No Other Fees. Except for the fees in this Section, the charges in the addenda, and the charges in the Schedule of Damage and Service Charges (Addendum N), we will not charge you any fee for the services described in this Lease.

## 9. UTILITIES AND SERVICES

9.1. Included Services. Your installments include your share of water, sewer, trash collection, high-speed internet, and basic cable television for the Unit, and your share of electricity up to the allowance stated in the Utilities Addendum (Addendum B).

9.2. Electricity Overages. Electricity is billed to the Unit as a whole. Charges above the allowance are divided among the residents of the Unit as the Utilities Addendum states.

9.3. Interruptions. We are not liable for any interruption of a utility or service caused by the utility provider, by weather, or by repairs, and your rent will not be reduced because of it, unless the interruption is caused by our negligence or Florida law provides otherwise. We will act with reasonable diligence to restore any service within our control.

## 10. OCCUPANCY AND GUESTS

10.1. Who May Live in the Bedroom. Only you may live in the Bedroom. No one else may occupy the Bedroom or the Unit as a resident unless they have signed a lease with us.

10.2. Guests. You may have guests in the Unit, subject to the rights of your Roommates and to the Community Policies (Addendum A), which limit overnight guests. You are responsible for the conduct of your guests anywhere in the Community, and for any damage they cause.

10.3. Guest Access. Guests must be with you while in the amenity areas of the Community. You may not give your key fob, access card, or gate code to any guest.

## 11. ROOMMATE MATTERS

11.1. Shared Areas. You and your Roommates share the common areas of the Unit and are expected to keep them clean and to respect each other's privacy, property, and study time.

11.2. Roommate Conflicts. If a conflict arises with a Roommate, you should first try to resolve it directly. If you cannot, you may ask the Community's residence life staff to arrange a mediation meeting. We are not required to move you or a Roommate because of a disagreement, but we may do so under Section 2.4.

11.3. Roommate Agreement. Within fourteen (14) days after the Move-In Date, the residents of the Unit should complete the Roommate Agreement form, which covers cleaning schedules, quiet study times, shared purchases, and use of the common areas. The Roommate Agreement is between the Roommates only and does not change this Lease.

## 12. MOVE-IN AND INSPECTION

12.1. Move-In Inspection. At move-in you will receive a Move-In and Move-Out Inspection Checklist (Addendum M). You must note any damage or defect in your Bedroom, bathroom, and the Unit's common areas and return the signed checklist to the leasing office within forty-eight (48) hours after you receive your keys. Any item not noted will be treated as being in good condition at move-in.

12.2. Keys and Access Devices. At move-in you will receive one Bedroom key, one Unit key, one mailbox key, and one access card for the building and amenity areas.

12.3. Condition. You accept the Bedroom and the Unit in their condition at move-in, subject to the items noted on your checklist and to our duties under Section 83.51, Florida Statutes.

## 13. REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE REQUESTS

13.1. How to Ask for a Repair. All requests for repairs and maintenance must be submitted through the Blue Heron resident portal or mobile app, under "Maintenance Requests." Requests made by phone, by text, or in person at the leasing office will be entered in the portal by our staff, but you should check that your request appears there. A request submitted through the portal is written notice to us of the condition described in it.

13.2. Emergencies. An emergency is a condition that threatens life, health, or property, such as a fire, a flood or active water leak, a sewer backup, a gas odor, a power outage in the Unit, a broken exterior door lock, or loss of air conditioning when the outside temperature is above 85 degrees. For an emergency, call our 24-hour emergency maintenance line at (352) 555-0147 at once, and then submit a portal request. In a fire or a medical emergency, call 911 first.

13.3. Response Times. We will respond to emergency requests as soon as reasonably possible, and in any case within twenty-four (24) hours. We will respond to other requests within five (5) business days. Response times may be longer during the move-in and move-out periods and after a hurricane or other storm.

13.4. Our Repair Duties. We will maintain the Unit and the Community as required by Section 83.51, Florida Statutes, and will repair the heating and air-conditioning system, plumbing, electrical system, appliances, and furniture we provide, except where the repair is needed because of the misuse, negligence, or intentional act of you or your guests.

13.5. Your Duties. You must promptly report any water leak, mold, pest, broken smoke detector, or other condition that needs repair. You must not make repairs yourself or hire anyone to make repairs, except to change light bulbs and air-conditioning filters supplied by us. You will pay the reasonable cost of any repair needed because of damage caused by you or your guests, other than normal wear and tear, using the charges in Addendum N where a charge is listed.

13.6. Filters and Light Bulbs. We supply replacement air-conditioning filters and light bulbs at the leasing office at no charge. The residents of the Unit are responsible for changing the air-conditioning filter at least once every month.

## 14. OUR ENTRY INTO THE UNIT

14.1. Reasons for Entry. We may enter the Unit and your Bedroom to inspect them, make necessary or agreed repairs, perform preventive maintenance, change air filters, test smoke detectors, treat for pests, or show the Unit to prospective residents, contractors, insurers, or lenders.

14.2. Notice. Except in an emergency, we will give you at least twenty-four (24) hours' notice before entering, by email to the address in your resident portal account or by a written notice left on the Unit's front door, and we will enter only between 7:30 a.m. and 8:00 p.m.

14.3. Maintenance Requests. If you submit a maintenance request, you authorize us to enter the Unit and your Bedroom to make the repair during the hours in Section 14.2 without further notice.

14.4. Emergencies and Absence. We may enter at any time without notice in an emergency or to protect or preserve the Community. We may also enter without notice if you are absent from the Unit for one-half of a rental installment period without telling us, as Florida law permits.

14.5. Inspections. We may inspect each unit once each semester, and before move-out, to check for damage, safety hazards, and compliance with this Lease. We will leave a written notice in the Unit after each entry stating the date, time, and reason for the entry.

## 15. ANIMALS

15.1. No Animals Without Approval. You may not keep any animal in the Unit, your Bedroom, or anywhere in the Community, even temporarily, unless we have approved it in writing under the Animal Addendum (Addendum C) or as a reasonable accommodation for a person with a disability.

15.2. Unauthorized Animals. If we find an unauthorized animal, we may charge you $150.00 for each violation, and the cost of cleaning, deodorizing, and pest treatment, in addition to any other remedy this Lease or Florida law allows.

15.3. Assistance Animals. Service animals and support animals are not pets. A request for a reasonable accommodation for an assistance animal may be made at any time at the leasing office or through the resident portal, and no pet fee or pet rent will be charged for an approved assistance animal.

## 16. PARKING

16.1. Permits Required. Vehicles parked in the Community must display a valid Archer Commons parking permit. Permits are offered for the garage and for the surface lots, on the terms and at the prices stated in the Parking Addendum (Addendum D).

16.2. No Guarantee. A parking permit does not guarantee a particular space. Parking is at your own risk, and we are not responsible for damage to or theft from any vehicle.

16.3. Towing. Vehicles without a valid permit, parked in a fire lane, parked in a reserved or handicapped space without authorization, or inoperable may be towed at the vehicle owner's expense by the towing company posted at the Community entrances.

## 17. RENTER'S INSURANCE

17.1. Insurance Required. You must keep renter's insurance in force throughout the Lease term as stated in the Renter's Insurance Addendum (Addendum G).

17.2. Our Insurance. Our insurance does not cover your belongings or your liability to others. You are responsible for any loss of or damage to your personal property, including loss caused by fire, theft, water, wind, or storm, unless the loss is caused by our negligence.

## 18. SMOKING

18.1. Smoke-Free Community. The Community is smoke-free. The rules on smoking and vaping are in the Smoke-Free Addendum (Addendum J).

## 19. CONDUCT AND COMMUNITY POLICIES

19.1. Policies. You, your guests, and anyone else in the Community with your permission must follow the Community Policies (Addendum A), as we may reasonably change them from time to time after giving you thirty (30) days' written notice. A change will not increase your rent.

19.2. Conduct. You must not disturb the peace, quiet, or study of your Roommates or other residents, must not commit or allow any illegal act in the Community, and must not threaten, harass, or endanger any resident, guest, or employee.

19.3. Students' Code of Conduct. Conduct in the Community may also be subject to your school's student code of conduct. We may report serious violations to the school as its policies allow.

## 20. ALTERATIONS AND DECORATIONS

20.1. No Alterations Without Consent. You may not paint, wallpaper, install fixtures or shelving, change or add locks, install a security camera or doorbell camera, or make any other alteration to the Unit without our written consent.

20.2. Decorations. You may hang pictures and posters in your Bedroom using small nails, push pins, or removable adhesive strips. You may not use tape on painted walls or hang anything from the ceiling or the fire sprinkler heads. You may display one United States flag as Florida law allows.

20.3. Restoring the Unit. At move-out you must remove your decorations and restore the walls to their move-in condition, except for small nail and pin holes, which are normal wear and tear.
