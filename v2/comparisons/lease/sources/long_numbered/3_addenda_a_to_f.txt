# ADDENDUM A
# COMMUNITY POLICIES

These Community Policies are part of the Individual Lease Agreement for Archer Commons Student Residences. They apply to every resident and to every guest. A violation of these policies is a violation of the Lease.

## A-1. Quiet and Study

A-1.1. Quiet Hours. Quiet hours are from 10:00 p.m. to 8:00 a.m. Sunday night through Thursday night, and from 12:00 midnight to 9:00 a.m. on Friday night and Saturday night. During quiet hours, sound from your Unit, including music, television, and voices, must not be heard outside the Unit or in another resident's bedroom.

A-1.2. Courtesy Hours. At all other times, residents must keep noise at a level that does not disturb other residents.

A-1.3. Final Exams. During the two weeks of final examinations each semester, as posted by the Community, quiet hours are in effect twenty-four (24) hours a day.

A-1.4. Musical Instruments. Instruments and amplified sound may be played only in the Unit and only outside quiet hours, at a volume that cannot be heard in another unit.

## A-2. Guests

A-2.1. Overnight Guests. A guest may stay overnight in the Unit no more than three (3) consecutive nights and no more than six (6) nights in any calendar month, unless Manager gives prior written consent. Every Roommate must agree before any guest stays overnight.

A-2.2. Guest Registration. A guest staying overnight must be registered with the leasing office or through the resident portal before 10:00 p.m. on the first night of the stay.

A-2.3. Number of Guests. No more than two (2) guests per resident may be in the Unit at one time, and no more than eight (8) people in all may be in any Unit at one time.

A-2.4. Responsibility. A resident is responsible for the conduct of each of the resident's guests and for any damage a guest causes. A guest who violates these policies may be asked to leave the Community.

## A-3. Parties and Gatherings

A-3.1. No Large Gatherings. Gatherings larger than the limits in Section A-2.3 are not allowed in any Unit, on any balcony, or in any common area unless Manager has approved a reservation.

A-3.2. Alcohol. Residents and guests under 21 years of age may not possess or drink alcohol anywhere in the Community. Kegs, beer balls, drinking games, and open containers in common areas are not allowed for anyone.

A-3.3. Drugs. Illegal drugs and drug paraphernalia are not allowed anywhere in the Community.

## A-4. Balconies and Windows

A-4.1. Balconies. Only outdoor furniture designed for patio use may be kept on a balcony. Bicycles, laundry, towels, trash, coolers, and indoor furniture may not be kept on a balcony.

A-4.2. Grills and Open Flames. Charcoal grills, gas grills, and any other open-flame cooking device may not be used or stored on a balcony or within 10 feet of any building. Residents may use the gas grills in the courtyard.

A-4.3. Windows. Nothing may be hung in or thrown from a window. Window screens must stay in place. Signs, flags (other than one United States flag as Florida law allows), and banners may not be displayed in windows or on balconies.

## A-5. Fire Safety

A-5.1. Candles and Incense. Candles, incense, oil lamps, and other open flames are not allowed in the Unit.

A-5.2. Cooking. Never leave cooking unattended. Turkey fryers are not allowed anywhere in the Community.

A-5.3. Fire Equipment. Tampering with smoke detectors, sprinkler heads, fire extinguishers, fire alarms, or exit signs is a serious violation and may lead to ending the Lease and to criminal charges.

A-5.4. Fire Alarms. When a fire alarm sounds, everyone must leave the building at once by the nearest exit and may not return until staff or the fire department says it is safe.

## A-6. Trash and Recycling

A-6.1. Trash. Bag all trash and take it to the trash compactor next to the parking garage. Do not leave trash in hallways, stairwells, or breezeways, or outside the Unit door. A charge listed in Addendum N applies to each bag of trash left outside a unit.

A-6.2. Recycling. Recycling containers are next to the trash compactor. Flatten all boxes.

A-6.3. Large Items. Furniture, mattresses, and other large items may not be left at the trash compactor. Contact the leasing office to arrange removal for a fee.

## A-7. Common Areas and Amenities

A-7.1. Pool. The pool is open from 9:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. daily from March 1 through October 31, weather permitting. There is no lifeguard on duty, and residents swim at their own risk. Glass containers and alcohol are not allowed in the pool area. Each resident may bring no more than two (2) guests to the pool, and the resident must stay with them.

A-7.2. Fitness Center. The fitness center is open twenty-four (24) hours a day to residents with an access card. Guests may not use the fitness center. Wipe down equipment after use, and wear athletic shoes.

A-7.3. Study Lounges. Study lounges are for quiet study. Group study rooms may be reserved through the resident portal for up to two (2) hours at a time.

A-7.4. Clubhouse. The clubhouse is open during office hours and for scheduled resident events. The game room closes at 11:00 p.m.

A-7.5. Hallways and Stairwells. Hallways, stairwells, and breezeways must be kept clear. Bicycles, shoes, and other personal property may not be left in them.

A-7.6. Bicycles. Bicycles must be stored in the bicycle racks next to each building or inside the resident's own Bedroom. Bicycles left in racks at the end of the spring semester without a current registration tag may be removed after fourteen (14) days' posted notice.

A-7.7. Laundry. Use only the washer and dryer in your Unit. Clean the dryer lint screen after each load. We are not responsible for clothing damaged by the machines unless the machine was not working properly and you had reported it.

## A-8. Security and Access

A-8.1. Doors and Gates. Do not prop open any exterior door, gate, or stairwell door. Do not let anyone you do not know follow you into a building or the parking garage.

A-8.2. Access Cards. Access cards are personal and may not be shared. Report a lost card at once.

A-8.3. Solicitation. Door-to-door sales and solicitation are not allowed. Report any solicitor to the leasing office.

## A-9. Vehicles

A-9.1. Speed. The speed limit in the Community is 10 miles per hour.

A-9.2. Repairs. Vehicle repairs, other than changing a flat tire or a battery, are not allowed in the Community.

A-9.3. Skateboards and Scooters. Skateboards, hoverboards, and electric scooters may not be ridden in buildings, in the parking garage, or in the pool area. Electric scooters and hoverboards may not be charged inside the Unit unless they carry a recognized safety listing.

## A-10. Changes to These Policies

A-10.1. Changes. Manager may make reasonable changes to these Community Policies by giving residents thirty (30) days' written notice. A change will apply to all residents equally and will not increase rent.

# ADDENDUM B
# UTILITIES ADDENDUM

This addendum is part of the Individual Lease Agreement between Archer Commons Owner, LLC and Riley M. Torres for Bedroom C, Unit 3108.

## B-1. Services Included in Rent

Your monthly installment includes your share of the following services for the Unit: water, sewer, trash collection, high-speed internet (wired and wireless), and basic cable television. We choose the providers of these services.

## B-2. Electricity Allowance

B-2.1. Allowance. Your installment also includes electricity for the Unit up to an allowance of $40.00 per bedroom per month, which is $160.00 per month for a four-bedroom Unit (the "Electricity Allowance").

B-2.2. Overages. If the electric bill for the Unit for any month is more than the Unit's Electricity Allowance, the amount over the allowance (the "Overage") will be divided equally among the residents of the Unit who held a lease for that month, whether or not each resident was present. Each resident's share of the Overage is Additional Rent and will be billed through the resident portal, due with the next monthly installment.

B-2.3. Vacant Bedrooms. If a bedroom in the Unit is vacant for a whole month, its share of the Electricity Allowance and of any Overage is paid by us.

B-2.4. Copies of Bills. You may see a copy of the Unit's electric bill for any month through the resident portal or at the leasing office.

B-2.5. No Credit for Low Use. If the Unit uses less electricity than the allowance, no credit or refund is given.

## B-3. Late Payment of Overages

An Overage share not paid by its due date is subject to the late charge for utility overages listed in Addendum N, and not to the late charges in Section 5 of the Lease.

## B-4. Conservation

To help keep electricity costs down, set the thermostat no lower than 72 degrees in cooling season, keep windows and doors closed while the air conditioning is running, and turn off lights and electronics when you leave. If the Unit's thermostat is set below 68 degrees, we may raise it to 72 degrees after giving notice by email.

## B-5. Internet Use

The internet service is for personal, non-commercial use. You may not run a server, resell the service, or use it for illegal downloading. We may limit or suspend access for a device that harms the network.

Resident's Initials: R.M.T.

# ADDENDUM C
# ANIMAL ADDENDUM

This addendum is part of the Individual Lease Agreement between Archer Commons Owner, LLC and Riley M. Torres for Bedroom C, Unit 3108. It applies only to pets. Assistance animals are covered by Section 15.3 of the Lease.

## C-1. Pet-Friendly Units

C-1.1. Designated Units. Pets are permitted only in units that Manager has designated as pet-friendly. Unit 3108 is a designated pet-friendly unit.

C-1.2. Approval Required. You may keep a pet only after Manager approves the pet in writing and you and each of your Roommates have signed the Pet Approval Form for that pet. A Roommate's refusal to sign means the pet is not approved.

## C-2. Animals That May Be Approved

C-2.1. Kinds of Pets. Manager may approve domestic cats and domestic dogs only. Birds, reptiles, rodents, ferrets, and farm animals will not be approved. Fish in one aquarium of 10 gallons or less do not need approval.

C-2.2. Number. No more than one (1) pet per resident and no more than two (2) pets in the Unit may be approved.

C-2.3. Size and Breed. A dog must weigh no more than fifty (50) pounds when fully grown. Manager will not approve any dog that has a history of biting or attacking people or other animals.

C-2.4. Age and Health. A pet must be at least one year old, spayed or neutered, current on vaccinations required by Alachua County, and, for a dog, licensed by Alachua County. Cats must be kept indoors.

## C-3. Pet Fee and Pet Rent

C-3.1. Pet Fee. You will pay a non-refundable pet fee of $300.00 for each approved pet before the pet is brought into the Unit. The pet fee is not a deposit and will not be returned.

C-3.2. Pet Rent. You will pay pet rent of $25.00 per month for each approved pet, beginning in the month the pet is approved. Pet rent is Additional Rent.

## C-4. Rules for Pets

C-4.1. Leash. Dogs must be on a leash and under your control at all times outside the Unit.

C-4.2. Waste. You must pick up after your pet at once and put the waste in the pet waste stations. A charge listed in Addendum N applies to each pet waste violation.

C-4.3. Restricted Areas. Pets are not allowed in the pool area, the fitness center, the study lounges, or the clubhouse.

C-4.4. Noise. Your pet must not disturb other residents by barking, whining, or other noise.

C-4.5. Leaving a Pet Alone. A pet may not be left alone in the Unit for more than twelve (12) hours, and may not be left in the Unit when you are away overnight unless a Roommate has agreed in writing to care for it.

## C-5. Damage and Removal

C-5.1. Damage. You are responsible for all damage, cleaning, deodorizing, and flea treatment caused by your pet, including damage in your Roommates' bedrooms and in the common areas.

C-5.2. Removal. If your pet causes damage, disturbs other residents, or violates this addendum, Manager may revoke its approval by written notice, and you must remove the pet within seven (7) days.

Resident's Initials: R.M.T.

# ADDENDUM E
# MOLD AND MILDEW ADDENDUM

This addendum is part of the Individual Lease Agreement between Archer Commons Owner, LLC and Riley M. Torres for Bedroom C, Unit 3108.

## E-1. About Mold

Mold is found almost everywhere, indoors and out. It grows where there is moisture. In Florida's climate, controlling moisture inside the Unit is the best way to prevent mold.

## E-2. What You Must Do

E-2.1. Ventilation. Use the bathroom exhaust fan during and for at least fifteen (15) minutes after each shower. Use the kitchen exhaust fan when cooking.

E-2.2. Air Conditioning. Keep the air conditioning running and set no higher than 80 degrees, even when you are away. Keep the air-conditioning closet door closed.

E-2.3. Moisture. Wipe up spills, condensation, and water on bathroom floors and counters. Do not leave wet towels or clothing in piles. Do not dry clothing on furniture or balcony rails.

E-2.4. Reporting. Report any visible mold larger than a few square inches, any water leak, any musty odor, and any air conditioning problem through the resident portal within twenty-four (24) hours after you notice it.

E-2.5. Cleaning Small Areas. You may clean small areas of mold on hard surfaces, such as a shower tile, with a household mold cleaner. Do not paint over mold.

## E-3. What We Will Do

When you report mold or moisture, we will inspect the area and take reasonable steps to correct the cause and remove the mold. If the Unit must be repaired for mold, we may move you temporarily to another unit at no cost to you.

## E-4. Responsibility

You are responsible for damage from mold caused by your failure to follow this addendum. We are responsible for mold caused by a leak or other condition we must repair under the Lease, once we know of it.

# ADDENDUM F
# BED BUG ADDENDUM

This addendum is part of the Individual Lease Agreement between Archer Commons Owner, LLC and Riley M. Torres for Bedroom C, Unit 3108.

## F-1. Inspection Before Move-In

We inspected the Unit before your move-in and found no bed bugs. If you find bed bugs within seven (7) days after the Move-In Date, we will treat the Unit at no cost to you.

## F-2. Preventing Bed Bugs

Inspect secondhand furniture, luggage, and clothing before you bring them into the Unit. Do not bring in mattresses, upholstered furniture, or rugs found on the street or in trash areas.

## F-3. Reporting

You must report any sign of bed bugs, such as bites, small blood spots on bedding, or live insects, through the resident portal at once. Do not try to treat bed bugs yourself.

## F-4. Treatment

F-4.1. Access and Preparation. You must allow access for inspection and treatment, and you must follow our written preparation instructions, which may include washing and drying bedding and clothing on high heat and bagging belongings.

F-4.2. Cost. We will pay for treatment unless an inspection shows that the bed bugs were brought into the Unit by you or your guests, in which case you will pay the reasonable cost of treatment of the Unit and of any neighboring unit.

Resident's Initials: R.M.T.
