# ADDENDUM G
# RENTER'S INSURANCE ADDENDUM

This addendum is part of the Individual Lease Agreement between Archer Commons Owner, LLC and Riley M. Torres for Bedroom C, Unit 3108.

## G-1. Insurance Required

You must keep a renter's insurance policy in force from the Move-In Date through the End Date. The policy must provide personal liability coverage of at least $100,000 per occurrence and must list Archer Commons Owner, LLC as an "interested party" so that we are told if the policy is cancelled.

## G-2. Two Ways to Meet the Requirement

G-2.1. Your Own Policy. You may buy a policy from any insurer licensed in Florida and upload proof of coverage to the resident portal before the Move-In Date.

G-2.2. Community Liability Program. If you do not provide proof of your own policy, you will be enrolled in the Community's resident liability program, which provides $100,000 of liability coverage for damage you cause to the Community. The charge is $14.00 per month, billed as Additional Rent. The program does not cover your own belongings.

## G-3. Proof of Coverage

If your policy is cancelled or ends, you must buy a new policy and upload proof within ten (10) days, or you will be enrolled in the Community liability program.

## G-4. No Coverage by Us

We do not insure your belongings. We strongly recommend that your policy also cover personal property against fire, theft, water damage, and storms.

Resident's Initials: R.M.T.

# ADDENDUM H
# LEASE TRANSFER ADDENDUM

This addendum is part of the Individual Lease Agreement between Archer Commons Owner, LLC and Riley M. Torres for Bedroom C, Unit 3108.

## H-1. What a Lease Transfer Is

A lease transfer replaces you with a new resident (the "Replacement") for the rest of the Lease term. It is the only way you may give up your Bedroom before the End Date, other than the Early Termination Addendum or as Florida law allows. Subletting is not allowed (Section 23.1 of the Lease).

## H-2. Requirements

H-2.1. Finding a Replacement. You may find your own Replacement, or you may ask us to list your Bedroom on our transfer list. We do not promise that we will find a Replacement, and we will lease vacant bedrooms in the Community before transfer-list bedrooms.

H-2.2. Approval. The Replacement must apply, meet our current rental standards, and be approved by Manager. A Replacement must also be acceptable to the residents of the Unit if the Unit is a same-gender unit under our roommate assignment policy.

H-2.3. New Lease. The Replacement must sign a new lease for the rest of the term at the current installment amount for your Bedroom, and must pay the security deposit, administrative fee, and amenity fee then in effect.

H-2.4. Transfer Fee. You must pay a lease transfer fee of $300.00 when the Replacement signs the new lease. If we found the Replacement from our transfer list, the fee is $450.00.

H-2.5. Your Account. All amounts you owe through the transfer date must be paid in full before the transfer is complete.

## H-3. When You Are Released

You are released from future rent under this Lease only on the date the Replacement's lease begins and you have returned all keys and access cards. Until then you remain responsible for all rent and other charges under this Lease.

## H-4. Your Deposit

Your security deposit will be handled under Section 83.49, Florida Statutes, as if you had moved out on the transfer date.

Resident's Initials: R.M.T.

# ADDENDUM I
# PACKAGE ACCEPTANCE ADDENDUM

This addendum is part of the Individual Lease Agreement between Archer Commons Owner, LLC and Riley M. Torres for Bedroom C, Unit 3108.

## I-1. Package Lockers

Packages delivered by carriers are placed in the smart package lockers in the clubhouse mail room. You will receive a text message or email with a code when a package arrives for you.

## I-2. Pickup

You must pick up your package within five (5) days. After five (5) days, packages are moved to the leasing office, and after fourteen (14) days they may be returned to the sender.

## I-3. Large and Perishable Packages

Packages too large for the lockers are held at the leasing office for pickup during office hours. We do not accept perishable food deliveries, flowers, or packages that require a signature or refrigeration.

## I-4. Limits on Our Responsibility

Accepting packages is a courtesy. We are not responsible for lost, stolen, or damaged packages unless the loss is caused by our gross negligence.

# ADDENDUM J
# SMOKE-FREE ADDENDUM

This addendum is part of the Individual Lease Agreement between Archer Commons Owner, LLC and Riley M. Torres for Bedroom C, Unit 3108.

## J-1. Smoking Not Allowed

Smoking is not allowed anywhere in the Community, including inside any unit, on balconies and patios, in the parking garage, in the pool area and courtyard, and within twenty-five (25) feet of any building entrance. "Smoking" includes cigarettes, cigars, pipes, hookahs, electronic cigarettes, and vaping devices, of any substance.

## J-2. Designated Area

Smoking is allowed only in the designated smoking area at the northeast corner of the surface lot, marked by a sign.

## J-3. Violations

The first violation will result in a written warning. Each later violation will result in a charge listed in Addendum N, and repeated violations are a material breach of the Lease. You are responsible for the cost of removing smoke odor and residue from the Unit and its furniture.

Resident's Initials: R.M.T.
