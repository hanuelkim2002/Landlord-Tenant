# ROOM RENTAL AGREEMENT

This agreement is between Patricia Nguyen (the "Landlord") and Samuel Okonkwo (the "Tenant"). The Landlord rents to the Tenant the back bedroom (Bedroom 3) of the house at 1419 NW 5th Avenue, Gainesville, Florida 32601, with shared use of the kitchen, living room, laundry room, and hall bathroom. Two other tenants rent the other bedrooms under their own agreements.

## Term

The Tenant may move in on August 10, 2026. This agreement ends on July 31, 2027.

## Rent

Rent is $650.00 per month, due on the 1st day of each month. The Tenant may pay by Zelle to the Landlord's phone number below, or by check made out to Patricia Nguyen. The Landlord does not accept cash. Rent for August 2026 is $460.00 because the Tenant moves in on August 10.

## Late Rent

If rent is not paid by the end of the 5th day of the month, the Tenant owes a late fee of $40.00. A check that bounces costs an extra $30.00.

## Security Deposit

The Tenant paid a security deposit of $650.00 when signing. The Landlord keeps the deposit in a separate account at Truist Bank in Gainesville that does not earn interest. The deposit is not the last month's rent. The Landlord will return it, or send written notice of any claim against it, as Florida law requires after the Tenant moves out. The Tenant should give the Landlord a forwarding address in writing.

## Utilities

The Landlord pays for water, sewer, trash, and internet. Electricity is in the Landlord's name, and the three tenants split the monthly electric bill equally. The Landlord will text each tenant a photo of the bill, and the Tenant's share is due with the next month's rent.

## Use of the House

The room is for the Tenant to live in, and no one else may live in it. Quiet hours are 11:00 p.m. to 7:00 a.m. every night. Each tenant cleans up after themselves in the kitchen and takes a turn putting the trash and recycling out on Tuesday nights. The Tenant may not paint, change the locks, or put holes larger than a small nail in the walls without the Landlord's written OK.

## Guests

A guest may stay overnight no more than 3 nights in a row and no more than 8 nights in a month. The Tenant is responsible for guests and must make sure they follow these rules.

## Pets

No pets of any kind may be kept in the house or yard, even for a short visit. The only exception is fish in one tank of 10 gallons or less, kept in the Tenant's room.

## Smoking

No smoking or vaping inside the house. Smoking is allowed only on the back patio, with the door closed.

## Parking

The Tenant may park one car in the driveway, in the space marked "3." No parking on the grass.

## Repairs

Tell the Landlord about any repair the house needs by text or email (contact information is below). For an emergency, such as a leak, no air conditioning in summer, or a sparking outlet, call the Landlord's phone and do not just text. The Landlord will try to fix ordinary problems within a week. The Tenant pays for repairs needed because of damage the Tenant or the Tenant's guests caused, other than normal wear and tear. The Tenant replaces light bulbs in the Tenant's room and the air conditioning filter when it is the Tenant's month on the chore list.

## Landlord's Entry

The Landlord may enter the shared parts of the house at any time to check on them or make repairs. The Landlord will enter the Tenant's room only for repairs or inspections, after giving at least 24 hours' notice by text, between 9:00 a.m. and 6:00 p.m., unless there is an emergency.

## Notices

Day-to-day messages may be sent by text or email. A formal written notice to the Landlord, such as a notice about the lease or about the deposit, must be delivered by hand or sent by mail to Patricia Nguyen, 2840 NW 16th Boulevard, Gainesville, Florida 32605. Written notices to the Tenant may be delivered to the Tenant's room or mailed to the house.

Landlord's phone: (352) 555-0198. Landlord's email: pnguyen.rentals@example.com

## Ending the Lease

This agreement does not renew on its own. If the Tenant wants to stay after July 31, 2027, the Tenant should tell the Landlord in writing by May 31, 2027, and the Landlord will say whether a new agreement is offered. At move-out the Tenant must leave the room clean, empty, and with all keys returned.
