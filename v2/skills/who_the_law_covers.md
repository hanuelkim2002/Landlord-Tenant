# Who the law covers

Rests on 83.42. Read it in full with get_statute_section before telling the tenant whether part II covers their home.

## College housing

- College housing may be outside part II. It does not apply to "Residency or detention in a facility, whether public or private, when residence or detention is incidental to the provision of medical, geriatric, educational, counseling, religious, or similar services." (83.42(1)). Settle this once for the matter, in this order, and never ask it twice:
  - The facts already hold school_housing, pending or confirmed: use it, and do not ask again.
  - Otherwise read the lease: who the landlord is, and whether living there is tied to a school, to being enrolled, or to services a school provides. The lease is clear only if nothing in it points to a school and nothing leaves doubt. Treat it as not clear if it has anything like: a clause on enrollment or attendance (the landlord may ask about it, or end the lease if the tenant is not enrolled, suspended, or expelled); a title or terms for students, or housing only for students; a school named as owner, manager, or partner; rooms assigned by a school; services a school provides (meals, advising, resident programs); a term set by the school year or semesters; or anything else that leaves doubt. If the lease is clear, say what it shows in one line inside another point of the answer, never a point of its own, cite the lease, and note it with note_fact as school_housing (tenant_words: "from the lease").
  - If the lease does not make it clear, ask the tenant one plain question: is the housing run by or for their school, or can they live there only while enrolled? Note the answer with note_fact as school_housing.

## The other four

83.42 also leaves out a buyer living in the home under a contract of sale (83.42(2)), "Transient occupancy in a hotel, condominium, motel, roominghouse, or similar public lodging, or transient occupancy in a mobile home park." (83.42(3)), a cooperative apartment held under a proprietary lease (83.42(4)), and a condominium unit its owner lives in (83.42(5)). Check these only when the lease or the facts point to one.

## When one applies

Say so plainly and cite the part of 83.42: part II's rules then do not decide the dispute, and what does is outside this tool. If the facts leave it in doubt, say so and answer both ways.
