# Court-approved landlord and tenant forms

Decisions of the Florida Supreme Court approving fill-in forms, including notices under part II, that
nonlawyers may help complete. The forms were drafted by the Bar. The Bar also prepared instructions,
whose publication the court authorized without passing on their correctness; the opinion does not say
whether a form's printed notes are part of those instructions.

## Cases

- [[Florida Bar re Approval of Forms (1991)]], Fla. 1991, the forms citing the 1990 statute: what did the court approve?

## How they relate

- What the court said about the instructions: "While we authorize the publication of the instructions and information sheet, we do not express an opinion on the legal correctness of either." ([[Florida Bar re Approval of Forms (1991)]] at 595)
- The forms cite the 1990 statute, for example: "SOURCE: Section 83.49(3)(a), Florida Statutes (1990)." ([[Florida Bar re Approval of Forms (1991)]] at 596) The deposit form's note gives 15 days; today's 83.49(3)(a) gives 30 (see [[Landlord's notice of claim on the deposit]]).
- The 2010 decision revising the forms, 50 So. 3d 503, is not in `cases/`.
