# Applying an amended statute

Cases on whether a new or amended provision of part II reaches a lease signed, or a dispute that arose,
before the provision took effect.

## Cases

- [[Department of Business Regulation v. Stein]], Fla. 3d DCA 1976: did the interest requirement reach deposits under leases signed before October 1, 1972?
- [[Xanadu of Cocoa Beach, Inc. v. Lenz]], Fla. 5th DCA 1987: did the 1983 version of 83.48 apply to a rental agreement signed before it took effect?

## How they relate

- Stein, on interest: "In making this determination, we recognize the presumption that statutes operate prospectively unless legislative intent to the contrary clearly appears." ([[Department of Business Regulation v. Stein]] at 207)
- Xanadu, on fees treated as costs, compares a different date: "the relevant analysis does not compare the effective date of the statute providing for court costs with the date of the contract, if any, which may be the cause of action in the particular litigation, but compares the effective date of the statute providing for court costs with the date of the accrual of the cause of action" ([[Xanadu of Cocoa Beach, Inc. v. Lenz]] at 520)
- Xanadu does not cite Stein.
