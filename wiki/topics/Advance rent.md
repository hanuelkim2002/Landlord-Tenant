# Advance rent

Cases on what counts as advance rent, and what becomes of rent paid ahead when the lease ends early.

Today's stored text:
- "“Advance rent” means moneys paid to the landlord to be applied to future rent payment periods, but does not include rent paid in advance for a current rent payment period." (Fla. Stat. 83.43(2))

## Cases

- [[Atlantis Estate Acquisitions, Inc. v. DePierro]], Fla. 4th DCA 2013: was a year's rent paid in one lump sum advance rent? Even if it were, did the landlord have to return the unused part? Could the tenants recover it as unjust enrichment?

## How they relate

- Atlantis read the definition as 83.43(9): "Section 83.43(9) defines “advance rent” as “moneys paid to the landlord to be applied to future rent payment periods, but does not include rent paid in advance for a current rent payment period.”" ([[Atlantis Estate Acquisitions, Inc. v. DePierro]] at 893) The same words are in today's 83.43(2), above.
