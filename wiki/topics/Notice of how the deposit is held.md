# Notice of how the deposit is held

Cases on the written notice a landlord gives of where and how the deposit or advance rent is held.

Today's stored text:
- "This subsection does not apply to any landlord who rents fewer than five individual dwelling units. Failure to give this notice is not a defense to the payment of rent when due." (Fla. Stat. 83.49(2))

## Cases

- [[Plakhov v. Serova]], Fla. 4th DCA 2012, reading the 2008 statute: did the landlord's failure to give the notice excuse the rent?

## How they relate

- The 2008 wording the court quoted: "[f]ailure to provide this [section 83.49(2) ] notice shall not be a defense to the payment of rent when due." ([[Plakhov v. Serova]] at 1223) Today's text, above, reads "is not a defense".
- Left open in Plakhov: "We do not reach the issue of a tenant's remedy where the landlord fails to comply with section 83.49(2), Florida Statutes (2008)." ([[Plakhov v. Serova]] at 1223)
