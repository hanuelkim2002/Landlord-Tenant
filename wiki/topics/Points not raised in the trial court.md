# Points not raised in the trial court

Cases on arguments a party did not raise in the trial court, for example in its answer or in the pretrial
stipulation, and then raised on appeal.

## Cases

- [[Plakhov v. Serova]], Fla. 4th DCA 2012: could the tenant argue on appeal that the lease was not in evidence, or that the landlord had not complied with the notice-of-claim requirement, when neither was raised below?
