# Punitive damages against a landlord

Cases on whether a landlord's breach of the rental arrangement can support punitive damages.

## Cases

- [[Guthartz v. Lewis]], Fla. 3d DCA 1981: can punitive damages rest on the landlord's breach of contract alone?
- [[Lewis v. Guthartz]], Fla. 1982: must tenants plead and prove an independent tort to recover punitive damages for a landlord's breach of contract?

## How they relate

- The Supreme Court answered the question the Third District certified: "We answer the certified question “yes” and approve the decision of the district court." ([[Lewis v. Guthartz]] at 223)
- Both read the claim as one on the FHA regulatory agreement, not the rental agreements: "The gravamen of the Tenants' claim was embedded solely in terms of the regulatory agreement." ([[Lewis v. Guthartz]] at 224)
