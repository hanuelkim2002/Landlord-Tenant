# Purcell v. Williams

First District, Judge Zehmer writing, on the sellers' appeal from a judgment that cancelled a contract to sell a house but denied them all money relief against the buyer's heirs. The appellees made no appearance.

## Facts

- "In January 1986 the Purcells made a written agreement to sell real property located in Washington County to Marequita D. Williams." (at 1081)
- A typewritten special clause of the agreement: "In case of default, the Landlord-Tenant Act will apply, as to a month-to-month tenancy and all monies paid shall be forfeited." (at 1081)
- "Purcell again requested that Williams obtain insurance on the property, but Williams never did so before March 14, 1986, when a fire destroyed the house and caused Williams’ death." (at 1082)

## Was the agreement a contract for deed governed by the rules for mortgages, despite its clause applying the Landlord-Tenant Act on default?

Topic: [[Whether there is a tenancy]]

Rule:
- "The trial court failed to recognize that the instrument in question is essentially a contract for deed which constituted a security transaction subject to the rules applicable to mortgages." (at 1082)
- "As this purported lease provision was invalid, it provided no basis for the trial court to construe the agreement to be a lease or rental transaction rather than a mortgage transaction." (at 1083)
- "Therefore, appellants were limited to the remedies generally available to mortgagees, that is, foreclosure by action and sale, or an action in personam on the debt secured by the mortgage." (at 1083)

Reasoning:
- "The provision of the agreement which states that upon default the landlord-tenant act would apply introduced an ambiguity that apparently led the trial judge not to construe this as a mortgage transaction." (at 1082)
- "The apparent intent of this provision was to provide the sellers the right to take immediate possession of the premises upon default and as a consequence enable them to avoid the protections to the purchaser accorded by the laws governing mortgages. Thus it was invalid under the decision in O’Steen." (at 1082)
- "Consequently, as between themselves, the seller and buyer are respectively accorded the same remedies as the creditor and debtor in a mortgage transaction." (at 1082)

## Could the trial court deny the sellers all money relief because they did not insure the house themselves after learning the buyer had not?

Rule:
- "The trial court erred in denying appellants all monetary relief for Williams’ breach of the agreement." (at 1082)
- "In short, there is no legal or factual basis for this defense." (at 1083)

Reasoning:
- "The record shows that the Purcells contacted the purchaser about this default, demanded that she cure it, and that the purchaser was in the process of doing so." (at 1083)
- "We have been directed to nothing in this record or to any legal authority that placed the Purcells under a duty to obtain the insurance coverage on the house in order to protect their rights against the purchaser for her default." (at 1083)

## Also said

- "In view of this disposition, we find it unnecessary to reach the issue regarding specific performance." (at 1083)

## Statute read

- The court quoted section 697.01(1), Florida Statutes (1985): "All conveyances, obligations conditioned or defeasible, bills of sale or other instruments of writing conveying or selling property, either real or personal, for the purpose or with the intention of securing the payment of money ... shall be deemed and held mortgages, and shall be subject to the same rules of foreclosure and to the same regulations, restraints and forms as are prescribed in relation to mortgages." (at 1082)

## Related

- Follows Mid-State Investment Corp. v. O'Steen, 133 So. 2d 455 (not in `cases/`): "In O’Steen this court, under circumstances not materially different from this case, held a contract for deed to be a mortgage subject to the rules of mortgage foreclosure." (at 1082)
- Cites with see also, on contracts for deed as mortgages Cain & Bultman, Inc. v. Miss Sam, Inc., 409 So. 2d 114 (not in `cases/`)
- Cites with see also, on contracts for deed as mortgages H & L Land Co. v. Warner, 258 So. 2d 293 (not in `cases/`)

## Disposition

"The judgment is REVERSED and the cause is REMANDED for further proceedings." (at 1083)
