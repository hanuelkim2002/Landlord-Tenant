# Florida Bar re Approval of Forms (1991)

Florida Supreme Court, per curiam, on The Florida Bar's petition to approve forms that nonlawyers may help
complete. A forms decision: the opinion approves forms and appends them. The stored text marks no page
after 596, so every quote from the appended forms is pinned there.

## What did the court approve?

Topic: [[Court-approved landlord and tenant forms]]

Rule:
- "We approve and authorize the publication of forms 2 through 17, as listed above." (at 595)
- "While we authorize the publication of the instructions and information sheet, we do not express an opinion on the legal correctness of either." (at 595)

Reasoning:
- "Rule 10-1.1(b) allows nonlawyers to assist in the completion of legal forms approved by this Court." (at 595)
- "Under this rule, oral communication between the nonlawyer and the person being assisted is “restricted to those communications reasonably necessary to elicit factual information to complete the form and inform the person how to file the form.”" (at 595)
- "The forms developed by the Bar are of a fill-in-the-blank type and require nothing more than the insertion of factual information." (at 595)

## Forms

The forms that bear on ch. 83, with the source line each prints:

- Notice of intention to impose claim on security deposit: "SOURCE: Section 83.49(3)(a), Florida Statutes (1990)." (at 596) Its printed note, the Bar's wording: "If the landlord does not send the notice within the 15 day period he cannot keep the security deposit." (at 596)
- Notice from landlord to tenant, termination for failure to pay rent: "SOURCE: Section 83.56(3), Florida Statutes (1990)." (at 596)
- Notice from tenant to landlord, withholding rent for failure to maintain the premises: "SOURCE: Section 83.56, Florida Statutes (1990)." (at 596)

## Disposition

"However, we reserve ruling on form 1, the Residential Lease (For A Term Not To Exceed One Year), pending oral argument on that proposed form, which has been scheduled for Monday, February 3, 1992." (at 595)
