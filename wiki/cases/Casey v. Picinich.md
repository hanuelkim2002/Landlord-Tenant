# Casey v. Picinich

Fourth District, per curiam, on the tenant's appeal; the tenant appeared pro se. One judge dissented in
part.

## Facts

- "Appellees have conceded that appellant is entitled to $54.38 as interest earned on his security deposit and $25 from the portion of the security deposit retained by them." (at 77)

## What follows from the landlord's untimely notice of claim?

Topic: [[Landlord's notice of claim on the deposit]]

Rule:
- "Appellees' untimely notice forfeited their right to impose any claim upon appellant's security deposit." (at 77)

Reasoning: none stated beyond the statute the court cites, 83.49(3)(a) as of 1987.

## Is the tenant entitled to costs?

Topic: [[Fees and costs in a suit over the deposit]]

Rule:
- "Therefore, we reverse and remand with directions to enter judgment in favor of appellant in the amount of ninety-nine dollars and thirty-eight cents ($99.38) and the amount of his apportioned costs, if any." (at 77)

Reasoning: none stated.

Dissent:
- "In my opinion the appellant is only entitled to the return of $45.00 ($25.00 plus $20.00; see opinion)." (at 77)
- "I am also not sure what “apportioned costs” the majority is approving. Whatever, I dissent as to the award of costs." (at 77)

## Related

- [[Durene v. Alcime]] (1984) also reads 83.49(3)(a) on a late notice. Casey does not cite it.

## Disposition

"We affirm as to all issues on appeal, except one." (at 77)
