# Walsh v. CAS, Inc

Fourth District, per curiam, on the tenant's appeal from the dismissal with prejudice of the wrongful eviction count of her counterclaim, which the trial court held barred by an earlier county court judgment of eviction for nonpayment of rent.

## Facts

- "In Count V, the tenant has interwoven several theories under the rubric of wrongful eviction." (at 562)

## Does a county court judgment of eviction for nonpayment bar the tenant's later claim that the eviction was wrongful?

Topic: [[Self-help and wrongful eviction]]

The issue in the court's words: "At issue in this appeal is whether Count V of appellant’s (tenant’s) counterclaim entitled “wrongful eviction” was properly dismissed because of the res judicata effect of a prior judgment of eviction, entered against the tenant in a county court eviction proceeding." (at 562)

Rule:
- "However, to the extent that the counterclaim contains allegations attacking the propriety of the county court eviction proceeding for nonpayment of rent, the lawful judgment of eviction bars the claim of wrongful eviction arising from the actual eviction." (at 562)
- "A tenant who has been lawfully evicted pursuant to statutory procedure is precluded, in a separate action, from attacking that eviction as wrongful." (at 562)

Reasoning:
- "An eviction, whether actual or constructive, must be wrongful or unlawful in order to be actionable." (at 562)

## Does the judgment of eviction also bar a constructive eviction claim based on code violations that came before the eviction action?

Topic: [[Constructive eviction]], [[Self-help and wrongful eviction]]

Rule:
- "To the extent that a portion of the count contains allegations of constructive eviction based on code violations which preceded the appellee’s (landlord’s) initiation of the county court eviction action, the action is not barred by the subsequent lawful eviction." (at 562)

Reasoning: none stated.

## Related

- Cites, on a constructive eviction claim that came before the eviction action Farm Maid Inc. v. Giller, 474 So. 2d 1278 (not in `cases/`): "the action is not barred by the subsequent lawful eviction" (at 562)
- Cites, on an eviction that must be wrongful to be actionable McCready v. Booth, 398 So. 2d 1000 (not in `cases/`): "An eviction, whether actual or constructive, must be wrongful or unlawful in order to be actionable." (at 562)
- Cites, on an eviction that must be wrongful to be actionable Sentry Water Systems, Inc. v. ADCA Corp., 355 So. 2d 1255 (not in `cases/`): "An eviction, whether actual or constructive, must be wrongful or unlawful in order to be actionable." (at 562)

## Disposition

"Accordingly, we reverse the order of the trial court dismissing Count V of the supplemental counterclaim with prejudice. We remand with directions to dismiss Count V without prejudice and with leave to amend only with respect to a claim for wrongful constructive eviction resulting from code violations." (at 562)
