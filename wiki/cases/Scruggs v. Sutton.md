# Scruggs v. Sutton

Third District (Cope, J.), on the owners' appeal from a fee award to the occupant who defeated their
eviction claim and won a resulting trust in the property.

## Must a party plead entitlement to fees under 83.48, which treats fees as costs?

Topic: [[Prevailing party fees under 83.48]]

Rule:
- "As we interpret Stockman, the claim must be pled, even if the applicable statute treats attorney's fees as costs." (at 854)
- "The fact that Sutton requested fees in his count for a resulting trust is insufficient to support an award of fees under section 83.48 for defeating the eviction action." (at 855)

Reasoning:
- The court quoted the Florida Supreme Court's Stockman v. Downs, 573 So. 2d 835 (Fla. 1991): "A party seeking attorney's fees pursuant to statute or contract must plead entitlement to such fees." (at 854)
- "Attorney's fees are not recoverable in an action for a resulting trust." (at 855)

## Disposition

"For the stated reasons, we reverse attorney's fees judgment and remand with directions to enter judgment for the Scruggs on the motion for attorney's fees." (at 855)
