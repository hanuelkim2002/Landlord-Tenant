# Mansur v. Eubanks

Florida Supreme Court, Justice Boyd writing, on the petition for certiorari of an injured neighbor and an incoming tenant to review a First District decision that affirmed summary judgments for the apartment's owners and Auto Owners Insurance Co. in a negligence suit over a gas explosion. Jurisdiction rested on conflict with a Third District decision; two justices concurred in result only.

## Facts

- "Sometime in mid-July, 1976, Marie Grigsby paid a deposit of $25 to Jean M. Barnett in order to reserve an apartment owned by Mrs. Barnett and Mary G. Eubanks." (at 1329)
- "In an effort to get the gas-fueled appliances (stove and water heater) operational, Grigsby and Mansur opened the gas line from outside the apartment." (at 1329)
- "Mansur struck a match and an explosion occurred." (at 1329)

## Does the landlord of a residential dwelling unit owe the tenant a duty to inspect and repair before the tenant takes possession, or does the rule of caveat lessee apply?

Topic: [[Landlord's liability for injuries on the premises]]

Rule:
- "We resolve the conflict by overruling Brooks v. Peters and adopting the Alexander rule." (at 1329)
- "We hold that the owner of a residential dwelling unit, who leases it to a tenant for residential purposes, has a duty to reasonably inspect the premises before allowing the tenant to take possession, and to make the repairs necessary to transfer a reasonably safe dwelling unit to the tenant unless defects are waived by the tenant." (at 1329)
- "This duty may be modified by agreement of the parties." (at 1330)

Reasoning:
- "We do not believe there are sufficient reasons to continue to completely insulate the landlord from liability." (at 1330)
- "We live in an age when the complexities of housing construction place the landlord in much better position than the tenant to guard against dangerous conditions." (at 1330)

## What duty does the landlord owe after the tenant takes possession?

Topic: [[Landlord's liability for injuries on the premises]], [[Landlord's maintenance duties under 83.51]]

Rule:
- "After the tenant takes possession, the landlord has a continuing duty to exercise reasonable care to repair dangerous defective conditions upon notice of their existence by the tenant, unless waived by the tenant." (at 1330)

Reasoning:
- "This latter duty corresponds to the statutory warranty of habitability noted by Judge Ervin in his concurring opinion below." (at 1330)

## Were the facts on possession and on the cause of the explosion in dispute, so as to stand in the way of summary judgment?

Topic: [[Landlord's liability for injuries on the premises]]

Rule:
- "The record shows, however, that there was a factual dispute as to this matter." (at 1330)
- "The actual cause of the explosion, however, and the questions of knowledge of and responsibility for the disconnected line were disputed matters which were never litigated due to the summary judgment." (at 1330)

Reasoning: none stated.

## Disposition

"The decision of the district court of appeal, affirming summary judgment, is quashed. We direct that the cause be remanded to the trial court for further proceedings consistent with this opinion." (at 1330)
