  facts that point is not settled." OK so not a straight answer, but at least it said where it hinges:

    determine which resident caused the damage" and it's split equally. So if the office "can't tell", I'm on the hook even tho it was Devin.

  * "Section 3.4 does not say what it takes for the landlord to 'determine' who caused the damage, so I cannot say which side you

- It never said anything about the washer STILL leaking (my other thing). I want that fixed. Maybe I'll ask.
- The "printed by the program" quotes at the bottom (statute 83.49, lease sections) were long but I get that they're the actual
