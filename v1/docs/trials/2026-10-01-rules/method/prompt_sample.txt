=== PLAYBOOKS section as the model sees it (claim scenario, two issues) ===


PLAYBOOKS:
issue: deposit_not_returned
guidance:
  questions:
    deposit_amount: How much was your security deposit?
    tenancy_terminated: What date did the lease end, or what date was it terminated?
    vacated: What date did you move out and return the keys?
    claim_notice_received: Have you received any written notice from the landlord about keeping part of the deposit?
    claim_notice_date: What date did you receive the landlord's written notice about keeping part of the deposit?
    forwarding_address_given: Did you give the landlord your new mailing address in writing?
    early_or_periodic: Did you move out before the lease end date, or was this a month-to-month arrangement?
    seven_day_notice_given: Before moving out, did you send the landlord written notice at least 7 days ahead, by
      certified mail or in person, with your new address?
  lease_checks:
    security_deposit: Confirms the amount and that it was called a deposit.
    nonrefundable_fees: Money labeled nonrefundable may fall under 83.491 instead. Do not treat it as outside 83.49
      without reading 83.491.
    forwarding_address_requirement: The landlord's notice goes to the "last known mailing address".
    move_out_requirements: Cleaning and carpet clauses are the usual basis of a claim.
    notices_to_landlord: Where the tenant's letter must be sent.
    joint_and_several_liability: Whether one roommate's damage can be charged against the whole deposit.
  evidence:
  - proof of deposit payment
  - move-out date (key receipt, walkthrough form, last utility bill)
  - move-in and move-out photos
  - forwarding-address notice with delivery proof
  - any landlord letters or emails, with envelope or full headers
  postures:
    core:
    - today's date
    - obligation identified
    - amount and return address
    - response deadline computed by the tool
    - delivery method
    preserve: States move-out date, amount, forwarding address. Asks for return. Cites nothing.
    assert: Adds the 83.49(3)(a) obligation, quoted.
    escalate: Adds the forfeiture consequence, quoted, and 83.49(3)(c) fee-shifting.
    notice_sufficiency: Sending this letter does not start any deadline that this tool computes for this issue.
      The only notice from a tenant that this tool tracks here is in Fla. Stat. 83.49(5), which says that "any tenant
      who vacates or abandons the premises before the expiration of the term specified in the rental agreement"
      "must give at least 7 days’ written notice by certified mail or personal delivery to the landlord before vacating
      or abandoning the premises". The same sentence also covers week-to-week, month-to-month, and other periodic
      tenancies. Whether it applies to you is not something this tool decides.
  failure_modes:
  - Using the lease end date as the termination date without also recording move-out.
  - Treating a "nonrefundable fee" as outside 83.49 without reading 83.491.
  - Skipping the 7-day notice question on an early move-out.
  - Computing any date outside compute_deadlines.
open_questions:
- id: termination_date_meaning
  sections:
  - '83.49'
  text: The clocks run from "termination of the rental agreement." The text does not say whether that is the lease
    end date or the move-out date. Record both; no cases loaded yet.


issue: deposit_claim_received
guidance:
  questions:
    deposit_amount: How much was your security deposit?
    tenancy_terminated: What date did the lease end, or what date was it terminated?
    claim_notice_dated: What date is printed on the landlord's notice?
    claim_notice_date: What date did you receive the landlord's written notice about keeping part of the deposit?
    claim_delivery: How did the notice reach you (certified mail, regular mail, e-mail, hand delivery, something
      else)?
    claim_amount: How much does the notice say the landlord is claiming from the deposit?
    claim_reason: What reason does the notice give for the claim? Copy the words from the notice.
    notice_states_objection_window: Does the notice tell you that you must object in writing within 15 days, and
      give an address to send the objection to?
    objected: Have you already sent the landlord a written objection to the claim?
    objection_date: What date did you send the objection?
    objection_delivery: How did you send the objection, and to what address?
  lease_checks:
    security_deposit: Confirms the amount and that it was called a deposit.
    deposit_deductions: What the lease says the landlord may deduct for. Compare against the reason in the notice.
    move_out_requirements: Cleaning and carpet clauses are the usual basis of a claim.
    nonrefundable_fees: Money labeled nonrefundable may fall under 83.491 instead. Do not treat it as outside 83.49
      without reading 83.491.
    notices_to_landlord: Where the tenant's objection must be sent under the lease, alongside the address given
      in the notice.
    joint_and_several_liability: Whether one roommate's damage can be charged against the whole deposit.
  evidence:
  - the notice itself, with the envelope or the full e-mail headers
  - the date the notice arrived (postmark, delivery scan, e-mail timestamp)
  - move-in and move-out photos
  - move-out walkthrough form, if any
  - receipts for cleaning or repairs the tenant paid for
  - the objection letter with proof of how and when it was sent
  postures:
    core:
    - today's date
    - the claim amount and reason as stated in the notice
    - the tenant's objection
    - the deposit amount
    - a return address
    - response deadline computed by the tool
    - delivery method
    preserve: States that the tenant objects to the claim, the amount, and the reason. Asks for return of the full
      deposit. Cites nothing.
    assert: Adds the 83.49(3)(b) sentence on the 15-day objection, quoted.
    escalate: Adds the 83.49(3)(c) sentence on court costs and attorney fees, quoted.
    notice_sufficiency: For this issue your letter is the objection itself. The statute sentence on objections is
      quoted above, and the tool has computed the 15-day date from the day you said you received the notice. Whether
      a letter sent after that date has any effect is not something this tool decides.
  failure_modes:
  - Computing the objection window from the date on the notice instead of the date of receipt, or the remit window
    from receipt instead of the date on the notice.
  - Treating the remit clock as unconditional. It comes from a sentence that begins "Unless the tenant objects".
  - Skipping the question of how the notice was delivered when it came by e-mail.
  - Computing any date outside compute_deadlines.
open_questions:
- id: receipt_date
  sections:
  - '83.49'
  - '83.505'
  text: The objection clock runs from "receipt of the landlord’s notice" while the remit clock runs from "the date
    of the notice". Record both dates. For e-mail, 83.505(4) says a notice "is deemed delivered at the time it is
    sent, unless the e-mail is returned to the sender as undeliverable"; the tool records how the notice arrived
    and does not decide which date counts.
- id: email_notice_conditions
  sections:
  - '83.505'
  text: 83.505(1) allows e-mail delivery only "if the parties have signed an addendum to the rental agreement".
    The tool records whether the tenant recalls such an addendum and does not decide whether the notice was validly
    delivered.
- id: objection_form
  sections:
  - '83.49'
  text: The statute's model notice says the tenant "must object in writing" and that the objection "must be sent
    to (landlord’s address)". No other delivery method or content requirement appears in the text. No cases loaded
    yet.
