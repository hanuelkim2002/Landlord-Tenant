     It says for me the leak, the black spots, my asthma and how long weigh in my favor, "The techs saying they need parts
     weighs the other way." So it feels 50/50 and I hate that I paid October rent.
   - 2. Not paying rent because of the problem: "not known yet". Something about a building or health code, calling "the

     And the statute needs "a separate addendum". "The tool found no addendum or liquidated damages term in your lease's
