## Entry 2 (the intake sheet, and then the program quit)
After my description it printed: "Part of what you described may not be covered by this tool yet (it has no playbook for
it). The tool continues with what it does cover." OK, a bit of a warning, fair.

Then the "sheet": ONE question. "[repair_not_made] This looks like: Something in the unit needs repair and the landlord
has not fixed it. Is that part of your situation? (yes/no)  suggested because of: the tenant's description."
I read that as "is something broken INSIDE MY APARTMENT?" My unit is fine (AC works, everything works). The broken stuff is
the pool, gym, study rooms, and the Wi-Fi. So I honestly typed "no". (Looking back: the Wi-Fi is "included in rent" and
I use it in my room, so I could see someone saying yes. But the question said "in the unit", and I would not have known
it matters. Nothing on screen told me that "no" would end everything.)

Answer: "Taken out of your matter; logged. None of the situations this tool covers applies, so it stops here. What you
described is saved in your matter's record." Then a list of 10 situations it covers (security deposit, entry without
notice, lease ending, noisy neighbor, 3-day notice, 7-day notice, rent increase, repair, moving out early). None of them
is my situation (amenities / ads / refund / petition). The program then said DONE. No chat prompt, no ">> ". I never got
to ask a single question.

What it did print: three lease sections "as printed in the lease":
- Section 6.2, Closure of Amenities, p.2: "Landlord may close any amenity ... The closure of an amenity does not entitle
  Resident to any reduction of Rent or any refund of the amenity fee."
- Section 3.3, Fees, p.1: amenity fee "is not refundable for any reason, including the closure of any amenity."
- Section 5.1, Included Services, p.2: "Rent includes water, sewer, trash removal, high-speed internet (Wi-Fi), and basic
  cable television."
Then: "This tool has no playbook for this situation, so it does not apply them to your facts."

How I feel: annoyed and a bit deflated. It dumped the two lease lines that say "no refund" at me and then said it
can't tell me what they mean. I now think my lease says I get nothing, but I don't know if that is really true (can a
lease just say that? do the ads matter? that is exactly what I asked). It also never answered "do the ads count" or
"can the 40 of us do anything together". The Wi-Fi one (5.1) is actually good for me, since the lease itself says Wi-Fi is
included, but the tool did not say what to do with that.
It also did not tell me what to do next. "If one of them is part of what happened, open this matter again (choose
existing) and describe that part" is the only instruction, and none of the ten really fits. Not sure if I should
re-describe my problem as a "repair" to get it to talk to me, which feels like gaming it.
In real life now: I'd show my roommates/the petition people the 5.1 Wi-Fi line, probably email the leasing office about
the Wi-Fi in writing, and google "Florida tenant amenities not provided" or ask the student legal services office. Lease
quotes were the only useful thing.
