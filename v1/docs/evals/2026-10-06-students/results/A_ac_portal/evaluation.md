## Plain summary (for the author)

- **Outcome:** The student left with a sendable email (assert-type letter, e-mail to office@magnoliarow.example, lease 7.1 quoted, respond by Oct 9) and learned of the lease's emergency-line clause (7.2, AC loss above 85 F outside). Those two were the real wins. Rent back got no usable answer (three routes, all "not known yet", plus lease 5.2 pointing the other way, no amount). The student never got a short list of what to do.
- **Biggest friction:** (a) the case-law block printed before the prose in the first two answers, none of it about AC, marked "[not verified]", skipped by the student; (b) long hedged prose ("not settled", "one reading / other reading", the 83.56(5)(a) waiver repeated in 3 answers) that never ends in advice for a student who does not want to end the lease or stop paying; (c) the answer opened on two options (ending the lease, not paying rent) the student never asked about and does not want; (d) the emergency line, the most actionable item, was buried mid-paragraph.
- **Specific confusions:** "the landlord's duty is met"; the portal request both "counts" and "does not count"; the bracketed note under the letter ("ask in the chat to have this applied" when the student does not want either intention); "No deadlines: none of the deadlines this tool computes applies..."; the "Written notice date: September 18, 2026" line in a letter when the tool itself said a portal request is not a notice; the "[Check: ...not in 83.51...]" lines (look like errors, e.g. a lease quote flagged against 83.51); letter-kind names preserve/assert/escalate; the structural/plumbing question for an AC problem; Enter not accepting the first suggested answer.
- **Gaps against what a student would expect:** no roommates (each on own lease, same problem), no escalation path past the leasing office, no plain "keep paying rent / eviction risk" statement, no help asking for a rent credit, no amount.
- **Honest strengths:** asked before drafting, remembered the student did not want to end the lease (kept escalate out), quoted the student's own lease, never claimed certainty it did not have, the letter was easy to revise, nothing was sent for the student.
