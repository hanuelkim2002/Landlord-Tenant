# In re Revisions to Simplified Forms (2010)

Florida Supreme Court, per curiam, on The Florida Bar's petition to amend residential lease forms and to
delete two. A forms decision. Stored from the Southern Reporter PDF: the opinion is in `cases/`; the
appendix of forms is not (see the scope note).

## What did the court approve?

Topic: [[Court-approved landlord and tenant forms]]

Rule:
- "We hereby approve the amendments to the following forms as shown in the appendix to this opinion" (at 503)
- "Finally, as recommended by the Board, we delete the following two forms" (at 503)
- "Accordingly, and upon consideration of the proposed revisions, we adopt same and approve the attached lease forms, effective upon the release of this opinion." (at 504)

Reasoning:
- "Under Rule Regulating the Florida Bar 10–2.1(a), Unlicensed Practice of Law, nonlawyers may assist persons in filling out legal forms approved by the Florida Supreme Court." (at 503)
- "Some of the amendments reflect statutory changes." (at 503)

## Also said

- "Added language is underlined and deleted language is struck through." (at 503)
- "We express no opinion as to whether the approved lease forms comport with current law." (at 503)
- "While we authorize the publication of the instructions, we do not express an opinion on their legal correctness." (at 503)

## Forms

- Approved as amended: "(1) Residential Lease for Single Family Home and Duplex (for a term not to exceed one year); (2) Residential Lease for Apartment or Unit in Multi–Family Rental Housing (Other than a Duplex) Including a Mobile Home (for a term not to exceed one year); and (3) Residential Landlord–Tenant Forms." (at 503)
- Deleted: "(1) Residential Lease for Unit in Condominium or Cooperative (for a term not to exceed one year) and (2) Residential Lease for Single Family Home and Duplex (for a term not to exceed one year) 1992 version." (at 503)
- The appended forms themselves are not in `cases/`.

## Related

- [[Florida Bar re Approval of Forms (1991)]] approved earlier landlord and tenant forms under a rule it numbers 10-1.1(b); this opinion names the rule 10–2.1(a). This opinion does not cite the 1991 decision.
- [[Wilson v. Terwillinger]] cites this decision for the form lease in its case: "See In Re Revisions to Simplified Forms Pursuant to Rule 10–2.1(A) of Rules Regulating The Fla. Bar, 50 So.3d 503, 503–11, 523–24 (Fla.2010)" ([[Wilson v. Terwillinger]] at 1123)
- Cites earlier revisions not in `cases/`: Fla. Bar re Revisions to Simplified Forms, 774 So. 2d 611 (Fla. 2000), and Fla. Bar re Revisions to Simplified Forms, 773 So. 2d 1062 (Fla. 1998).

## Disposition

"It is so ordered." (at 504)
