# Lakeway Management Co. of Florida v. Stolowilsky

Third District, per curiam, on the landlord's appeal from a bench-trial judgment that awarded rent only for the months the tenants occupied a leased condominium unit and denied the landlord attorney's fees. Irwin Stolowilsky appeared for himself.

## Facts

- "The Stolowilsky’s leased a condominium at the Treasure Bay Club Condominium, North Bay Village, Florida, for a period of one year, November 15, 1985 to November 14, 1986." (at 950)
- "The Stolowilskys paid $600.00 monthly rent in December, 1985, and then failed to pay any rent until they vacated the premises in March of 1986." (at 950)
- "At the conclusion of a bench trial the trial court entered a judgment for the lessor, but only for the period of actual occupancy, having permitted an amendment at the outset of the trial to urge constructive eviction as a defense." (at 950)

## Could the tenants raise constructive eviction when their first written complaint came after the landlord's three-day notice?

Topic: [[Constructive eviction]], [[Tenant's notice of the landlord's noncompliance]]

The issue in the court's words: "Lakeway urges that the trial court erred in allowing appellees to raise the defense of constructive eviction, despite the fact that no such defense had been pleaded" (at 950)

Rule:
- "We therefore find that the trial court erred in permitting this defense to be raised." (at 951)

Reasoning:
- "Only after the lessor sent a three-day notice pursuant to Section 83.20(2), Florida Statutes (1985) did the tenant make a complaint in writing which would meet the condition precedent to urging constructive eviction. See Section 83.60(1), Florida Statutes (1985)." (at 951)

## Should the landlord have been awarded attorney's fees under the lease?

Topic: [[Fee provisions in a contract or lease]]

Rule:
- "The lessor having prevailed and the lease providing for attorney’s fees in such instances, the trial court should have awarded such a fee." (at 951)
- "We therefore return the matter to the trial court for trial on the issues as made by the pleadings, with instructions that if the lessor prevails it be awarded attorney’s fees pursuant to the lease." (at 951)

Reasoning: none stated beyond the lease provision and the cases it cites.
