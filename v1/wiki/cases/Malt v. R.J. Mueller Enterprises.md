# Malt v. R.J. Mueller Enterprises

Fourth District, Judge Beranek writing, on the landlord's appeal and the tenant's cross appeal from a circuit court judgment over a warehouse lease. The landlord sued to evict; the tenant counterclaimed for reformation of the lease and for damages, and the trial court found for the tenant.

## Facts

- "The building in question was a warehouse which the tenant (Mueller) rented for $38,500 per year." (at 1175)
- "At the same time a sublease was agreed to whereby the landlord (Malt) was to sublease from the tenant (Mueller) 2,000 square feet of the same building for $2,400 per year." (at 1175)
- "The tenant claimed the landlord represented the building to be 22,000 square feet but that immediately upon taking possession, the tenant realized the building was smaller." (at 1176)

## Did the trial court err in refusing to evict the tenant?

Topic: [[Eviction procedure]]

Rule:
- "Under these circumstances, the landlord was not entitled to insist upon eviction by strictly applying the lease." (at 1176)
- "We conclude that the trial court did not err in refusing the landlord’s request for eviction." (at 1176)

Reasoning:
- "From the beginning, both parties engaged in disputes over issues of space, adjustments, rent, acceptance of back rent, and other breaches." (at 1176)
- "The landlord accepted late rent payments from the tenant and was also found to be in substantial breach of the sublease." (at 1176)
- "The landlord admitted never paying any rent on this sublease and the trial court could, thus, have properly considered those as equitable defenses to eviction." (at 1176)

## Was the lease properly reformed on the ground of mutual mistake about the size of the building?

Topic: [[Interpreting the lease]]

Rule:
- "Here, the tenant offered clear and convincing evidence of a mutual mistake, the size of the building, and the trial court properly reformed the contract." (at 1176)

Reasoning:
- "A court will reform a contract if it fails to express the parties’ intentions because of fraud, mutual mistake, accident or inequitable conduct." (at 1176)

## On the tenant's cross appeal, were the damages for breach of the sublease and for overpaid rent too low?

Topic: [[Tenant's damages for the landlord's breach]]

Rule:
- "Under the complex facts of this case, we conclude that the amount of these damages was in dispute and that the trial court did not err in the amounts actually awarded." (at 1176)

Reasoning:
- "The facts were complex and conflicting and after reviewing the briefs, transcript and evidence, we cannot say that the court erred in deciding these conflicts in favor of the tenant." (at 1176)

## On the tenant's cross appeal, was the attorney's fee award too low?

Topic: [[How a fee award is computed]]

Rule:
- "The award of attorney’s fees has not been demonstrated to have constituted an abuse of the trial court’s discretion and it is therefore affirmed." (at 1176)

Reasoning: none stated.
