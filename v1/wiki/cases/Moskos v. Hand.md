# Moskos v. Hand

Fourth District, Judge Walden, on the tenant's appeal from a judgment on the pleadings in the landlords' eviction suit. The trial court found the lease void for nonpayment of rent and the tenant a tenant at sufferance, and denied the landlords' request for immediate possession.

## Facts

- "Plaintiffs, the landlords, based their petition upon a failure of the defendant, tenant, to pay the December rent when due." (at 796)
- "The defendant’s sworn answer reflected that he had paid and the landlords had accepted the next month’s rent (January and that he had tardily tendered the December rent in the form of a Cashier’s Check which the landlords held but refused to negotiate." (at 796)

## Did the tenant's answer, saying the landlords accepted the next month's rent, raise waiver and estoppel issues that barred a judgment on the pleadings?

Topic: [[Accepting or refusing rent]]

Rule:
- "We believe the content of foregoing pleadings was of such nature as to make a justiciable issue in the areas of waiver and estoppel adequate to bar a judgment on the pleadings." (at 796)

Reasoning:
- "As a general rule a lessor is estopped to assert a forfeiture for a breach of a condition in a lease, or he waives his right to such a forfeiture, where after the breach he accepts rent from his tenant with knowledge or full notice thereof." (at 796)

## Was the three-day notice needed when the tenant had agreed in the lease to waive all notice?

Topic: [[Waiving the statutory notice in the lease]], [[Three-day notice for nonpayment of rent]]

Rule:
- "The tenant’s plaint that he did not receive the three days notice provided in Section 83.20(2), F.S.1969, F.S.A., is without merit." (at 796)
- "Here the notice was not necessary because the tenant in the lease expressly waived all notice required by law." (at 796)

Reasoning:
- "Such a lease provision takes precedence over the statute." (at 796)

## Disposition

"The judgment appealed is reversed and the case remanded for proceedings consistent herewith." (at 796)
