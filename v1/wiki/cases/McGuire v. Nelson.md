# McGuire v. Nelson

Fifth District, Judge Cobb, on the tenant's petition for review of a circuit court order, entered in its appellate capacity, that dismissed her appeal from an eviction judgment as frivolous and awarded the landlord a fee under 57.105. The opinion was amended on September 24, 1980.

## Facts

- "However, petitioner failed to deposit the disputed accrued rent into the registry of the court as required by section 83.60(2), Florida Statutes (1979)." (at 43)
- "In the second point, petitioner contended, for the first time, that section 83.60(2), Florida Statutes (1979) denies access to the courts and is therefore unconstitutional." (at 43)
- "The circuit court found that the appeal failed to raise any justiciable issue of law or fact and granted both motions." (at 43)

## Did the circuit court depart from the essential requirements of the law by dismissing the tenant's appeal as raising no justiciable issue?

Topic: [[Rent paid into the court registry]], [[Appeals in eviction cases]]

Rule:
- "We think the circuit court improperly avoided its responsibility to review the county court’s amended final judgment." (at 43)
- "While we express no opinion on the correctness of the petitioner’s argument, we do not believe that the appeal totally failed to raise a justiciable issue and we think that by denying the petitioner the opportunity to have her appeal heard on the merits, the circuit court departed from the essential requirements of the law." (at 43)

Reasoning:
- "Since the circuit court was sitting as the appellate court, we cannot review the merits of the case as a second appeal." (at 43)
- "The petitioner must demonstrate to this court that the circuit court exceeded its jurisdiction or departed from the essential requirements of the law in its order or proceedings." (at 43)
- "In this case, the merits were never reached." (at 43)

## Disposition

"QUASHED and REMANDED." (at 43)
